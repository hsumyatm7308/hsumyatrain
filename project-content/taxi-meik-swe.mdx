## What it does

Taxi Meik Swe is a car rental marketplace for taxi drivers in Myanmar. It connects owners who have unused taxi-ready cars with drivers who want to earn money as taxi drivers but don't own a car.

The company acts as an intermediary, supporting the rental process through verification, deposits, payments, inspections, and dispute management.

## Who it's for

- **Drivers:** People who want to rent a taxi-ready car and earn money by driving.
- **Car owners:** People who have an unused taxi-ready car they want to make available for rent.
- **The company:** The intermediary that helps manage the rental process between owners and drivers.

## Project background

Taxi Meik Swe was built during the One Project One Week hackathon. I worked on the project as a developer.
