## What it does

AURA is an AI-assisted platform for managing interviews and candidate selection across jobs, hackathons, and volunteer programs. It helps teams review applications, coordinate interviews, and evaluate candidates against clear criteria. AI supports the process by organizing information and surfacing evidence, while people make the final decisions.

## Features

- **Application screening:** Compare applications and CVs with selection criteria to create shortlists with explainable results.
- **Live interview support:** Transcribe interviews and highlight candidate strengths and relevant evidence as the conversation happens.
- **Interview scheduling:** Suggest interview times based on the interviewers’ availability.
- **Structured evaluations:** Score candidates against predefined rubrics and show the evidence behind each recommendation.
- **Candidate communication:** Prepare tailored emails for interview invitations, next steps, and rejections.
