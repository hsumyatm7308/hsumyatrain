## What it does

My dad often forgot the groceries my mum had asked him to pick up, so I built ZayList to make shopping lists easier for the whole household to share and use. Built with React and Vite, the app lets family members add items by typing or voice, switch to a focused shopping view, and keep track of what they spend. It can also be packaged as a mobile app for Android and iOS with Capacitor.

## Features

- **Shared household list:** Family members can use a shared board to keep grocery items in one place.
- **Quick item entry:** Add an item with its category, quantity, and an optional note. Voice-to-text makes it quicker to add or edit items.
- **Shopping mode:** Focus on items that still need to be bought and check them off as you shop.
- **Price reminders:** Checked items stay on the list until a price is recorded. Once an item has a price, it moves out of the main list after 24 hours.
- **Spending insights:** Review monthly spending, weekly trends, and totals by category.
